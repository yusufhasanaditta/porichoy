# Task: audit the content of a professional website

You get the text of up to 6 pages from one website (each starts with "=== PAGE: url"), plus the homepage title and meta description.
Write every human-readable field in {{lang_name}}. Keep quotes exactly as they appear on the page.

1. checks (quote the most relevant words and give the page_url):
   - who: it is clear who is behind the site (real name or business name), what they do and for whom.
   - proof: there is proof of experience, such as case studies, portfolio items, testimonials, client names or results.
   - contact: there is a clear way to get in touch (email, phone, form or booking link) and a location or service area.
   - recent: there are signs of recent, dated work or activity.

2. findings: the 3–6 most important content problems. quote must be exact words copied from that page's text (max 200 characters).
   Focus on what stops a visitor from trusting or hiring them. Don't repeat technical SEO issues (titles, tags, speed).

3. suggestions: a better homepage title (30–60 characters, name + main service), meta description (70–160 characters),
   h1, and the facts for structured data (entity type, name, job title, description). Use only facts from the pages.
